# e-plantShopping

## Paradise Nursery

Paradise Nursery is a React-based online plant shopping application that allows users to browse a variety of houseplants and add them to a shopping cart.

### Features

* Landing page with company information and a Get Started button.
* Plant listing page with multiple plant categories.
* Plant cards with images, names, descriptions, and prices.
* Add plants to the shopping cart.
* Dynamic shopping cart quantity.
* Increase and decrease plant quantities.
* Remove plants from the cart.
* Display the total number of plants and total cost.
* Continue Shopping and Checkout buttons.
* Redux Toolkit for shopping cart state management.

**Repository Name:** `e-plantShopping`
